Training Iterations: 1000
Evaluation Iterations: 20
Evaluation Interval: 100
Dropout: 0.1

LOSS RESULTS
------------
Initial Training Loss: 4.3086
Final Training Loss:   2.0549

Initial Validation Loss: 4.3154
Final Validation Loss:   2.0933

The final train-validation gap remained relatively small in this experiment,
while both losses decreased substantially during training.

BIGRAM VS MINIGPT
-----------------
The Bigram baseline generated mostly random-looking character sequences because
it uses only the current token. MiniGPT produced more structured text by using
preceding context through causal self-attention.
